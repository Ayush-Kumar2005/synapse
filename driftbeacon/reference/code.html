How DriftBeacon Detects Powertrain Drift

OBD-II Check Engine lights only illuminate after emissions thresholds fail or components break. DriftBeacon continuously compares real-world recorded driving against powertrain model baselines to uncover subtle fuel waste and physical drag weeks in advance.

1

Drive: Passive Background Recording

Continuous Telemetry

During daily trips, fuel burn rate, vehicle speed, load, and ambient temperature are recorded into baseline bins. Non-intrusive statuses keep the driver informed.

Zero annoying chime alarms during regular driving.
2

Analysis: Answer "What Changed?"

Divergence Isolation

Isolates exact operating regimes (e.g. steady cruise vs idle) where observed fuel exceeds expected L/h, differentiating driving behavior from mechanical resistance.

Historical multi-trip comparison across valid datasets.
3

Report: Driver-Controlled Technician Handoff

Handoff Ready

Generates an objective evidence dossier with speed, fuel delta, and telemetry coverage. Prompts the technician to inspect physical systems without declaring false part failures.

Certified mechanics retain full diagnostic discretion.
