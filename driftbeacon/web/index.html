How the DriftBeacon replay works

Follow the moving car, then compare clean, medium and high fuel drift. The expert system suggests a different inspection path for each outcome.

1

Drive: Follow the replay

Live demo view

Watch speed, RPM, load, expected fuel and estimated observed fuel progress together through the trip.

Pause, reset and change playback speed at any point.
2

Analysis: Answer "What Changed?"

Divergence Isolation

Explains why an alert appeared, what the expert system suggests, and the checks needed to confirm it.

Comparable-trip figures are labeled synthetic examples.
3

Report: Driver-owned summary

Editable draft

Combines read-only replay evidence with a title, observations and service history written by the driver.

Print or share only when you choose.
